What's inside

Which of these 15 leaks are costing your stores?

Each leak in the guide comes with the problem, five practical fixes, where AI helps, and a sourced industry stat.

  1. 1 Discounting Your Top SellersDiscounting what customers would have bought anyway is margin erosion in disguise.
  2. 2 Chasing Revenue, Not Profit DriversSales are an outcome you can't manage directly. Manage the leading indicators instead.
  3. 3 Training Customers to Wait for SalesConstant discounting builds discount dependency, not loyalty.
  4. 4 Losing Customers Without Knowing WhyOnly 1 in 26 customers complain. By the time sales dip, they've formed new habits elsewhere.
  5. 5 Top Stores Are Carrying the BusinessA few great stores hide a lot of average ones that quietly drag down sales and margin.
  6. 6 High Frontline Turnover Inflates Labor CostsToo much labour margin goes to hiring and retraining, and service suffers every day.
  7. 7 Labor Is Out of Sync with DemandOverstaffed when it's slow, understaffed at peak. Both hurt margin.
  8. 8 Missing High-Margin Sales OpportunitiesMost retailers chase more sales but spend little time growing their best-margin products.
  9. 9 Missing Cross-Sell and Basket GrowthLeaving buying patterns to chance means smaller baskets and missed profit.
  10. 10 Out-of-Stocks That Cost You CustomersYou can lose the whole basket, the next visit and the customer, not just one sale.
  11. 11 Dead Stock, Shrink and Returns Erode MarginExcess stock, slow movers, shrink and returns tie up cash and quietly erode margin.
  12. 12 Inefficient Processes Cut Sales per FTEEvery slow process erodes customer loyalty and lowers frontline productivity.
  13. 13 Unclaimed Vendor Marketing DollarsRetailers fund promotions from their own margin while vendors are willing to contribute.
  14. 14 Siloed Systems Don't Work TogetherFeedback, marketing, inventory, HR and ops each sit in their own system, hiding how they affect each other.
  15. 15 Discovering Problems Too LateMost reports tell you what happened last week or last quarter. Delayed visibility means delayed action.
