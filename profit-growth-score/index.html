How your score is calculated

One number out of 10. Eight places to look.

Each driver is rated from 1 to 5. Add up your totals, divide by 40, and multiply by 10 to get your Profit Growth Score.

The score tells you where you stand. The breakdown tells you where to focus: the 2 to 3 drivers that will deliver the biggest impact.

Example
Connect 6 /10
Engage 4 /10
Personalize 4 /10
Optimize 3 /10
17 ÷ 40 × 10 = 4.25 Profit Growth Score
