Have you ever walked into an ice cream shop and been overwhelmed by all the choices? Is this a bit like you in your business? Are you feeling overwhelmed about which action to take next? Not sure where to focus your attention, or which fire to fight first?

If you're an operations manager, you're not alone. Many of us feel overwhelmed and shut down with decision overload at one time or another. Time is the one resource that is finite, so you want to make sure you're spending it in a way that gets the best possible outcomes. How do you get clarity on what to do first? What's your most powerful next action to improve operations and financial results?

Don't decide in a vacuum. Take it to your customers and team members. Grow your sales and margins by doing the right things: the things that improve your customers' experience and your workplace experience.

**To prioritize your actions, you need the answers to these 6 questions:**

1. What do my customers love about us, and how do I strengthen that to grow?
2. Is anything frustrating my customers, and what action can I take to fix it?
3. What do my team members love about us, and how can I strengthen that?
4. Is anything stopping employees from delivering a great customer experience?
5. What can I do today to get my customers in more often and spending more?
6. What can I do today to build a workplace culture that attracts and keeps good people?

You don't have to solve every problem at once. Prioritize the actions that mean the most to your customers and teams.
