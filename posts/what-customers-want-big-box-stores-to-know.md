There seems to be a 💔 love-hate relationship between big box stores and their customers. Chatting with my friends and colleagues, almost everyone has a story about a big box store they frequent and a frustrating shopping experience.

They really appreciated the selection and staff, and at the same time, they wished it was easier to shop there. Sometimes they just weren't up for the challenge of walking the floor to find what they needed.

I created a LinkedIn poll to see how widespread this experience was and how big box retail chains could improve their shopping experience. Here's what I found out.

## How can big box chains resolve these issues and deliver exceptional customer service without increasing their labour budgets?

Big box stores have become a staple in the retail industry, offering a wide selection of products. However, customer service challenges often hinder the overall shopping experience, leading to customer frustration and lost sales. Let's explore some common challenges big box customers face and strategies to resolve them while improving associate efficiency, without increasing labour budgets.

### Challenge 1: It's hard to find the items customers want

**90% of customer help requests are "Where can I find . . ."**

The best way to resolve this is a customer service app accessible with QR codes placed in every aisle. Customers can self-serve to easily find the item they're looking for. Natural language processing (NLP) coupled with AI can identify the item's location and point the customer to the right aisle. Customers don't wander aimlessly looking for items. Store associates aren't constantly interrupted to help customers find things. Both customer satisfaction and staff efficiency go up, and stores stop losing sales on items not found.

### Challenge 2: It's hard to find associates for help

**49% of customers polled struggled to find store associates.**

When customers can't easily find an associate, they may get frustrated and leave. According to the poll results, this happens much more often than you would think.

To address this, big box stores can add a service request feature to their customer service app. Customers choose their preferred way to communicate: instant chat, phone call, or even video calling for remote stock checks. AI can route each request to the right department, streamlining the process.

This takes a huge load off the customer service desk, which no longer has to direct service requests manually. It reduces wait times at the desk, makes associates on the floor easier to reach, and improves overall productivity.

### Challenge 3: Inadequate staff knowledge

**28% of customers polled struggled to get answers to their questions.**

When customers find an associate, they expect them to be knowledgeable and able to provide accurate information. According to the poll results, this is often not the case.

To address this, big box stores can gather customer feedback right after service interactions. With AI, stores can identify where associates may need more training.

AI-powered training paths can deliver personalized, ongoing training as needed. This gives customers the answers they need, empowers associates, and reduces the burden on managers when service requests are escalated.

## Conclusion

By the very nature of their large footprints and great selection, big box stores face unique customer service challenges that affect where customers choose to shop.

Communication tools, NLP and AI make these challenges easy to resolve. Customer service apps accessed with QR codes give customers an easy way to find items and reach knowledgeable associates.

Real-time feedback after service experiences highlights issues for immediate resolution. Intelligent training paths build staff knowledge, so customers get the help they need without having to speak with multiple team members.

With these easy-to-implement strategies, big box stores can deliver exceptional customer service while increasing efficiency and making the most of their labour budgets.
