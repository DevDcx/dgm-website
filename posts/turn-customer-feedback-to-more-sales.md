### When you have your customers' attention, make it count

Every customer touchpoint is an opportunity to make a deeper, personal connection that leads to fanatical brand loyalty and more sales.

Digital comment cards give you essential insights into your customers' experience. They can also be used to improve social rankings, increase return visits and grow loyalty enrolment.

### 5 ways to increase customer retention, return visits and spend with comment cards

**1. Acknowledge and thank customers with "come back" offers**

Most business owners, marketing and customer success managers spend 80% of their time on reputation management. Resolving bad reviews and doing damage control when customers have a bad experience is essential. But it's the customers who give positive reviews who typically make up 80% of your revenue. And it costs 7 times more to get a new customer than to keep an existing one. To protect your valuable customer base from competitors, make sure they feel acknowledged and appreciated. Thank every customer with a response that fits their rating, and include a come back offer that gets them in one more time per period or increases their average basket size.

**2. Invite customers to share good reviews on social**

How easy do you make it for customers to share good reviews on their favourite social site? Can they copy their comments to social sites in just a couple of clicks? With the right incentives, you can get customers posting many more good reviews. Word of mouth is the most powerful form of marketing. Make it an essential part of your customer acquisition strategy.

**3. Act on feedback to improve operations**

There is nothing more frustrating for a customer than giving feedback, coming back, and seeing that nothing has been done. It's not enough to apologize for bad experiences. You need to turn comments into actions that improve operations. On the flip side, when customers see their ideas acted on, they feel like part of your company and become huge brand advocates.

**4. Grow loyalty signups with one-tap opt-in**

Your customers are giving you their contact information to receive a come back offer. What a great opportunity to invite them to get special perks all the time by joining your loyalty program. All it takes at that point is one tap to consent. Now you can use feedback and sales history to personalize communications and send targeted offers.

**5. Leave your customers with a reason to come back**

The last page of your comment card is an opportunity to advertise new products, events, specials and cross-promotions. Keep this page fresh with new content, so you can see what your customers respond to most. If customers opt in to your comment card on your payment device, this page can double as your screensaver.
