**Do your employees know more than you do?** When it comes to your customers and operations, chances are the answer is yes. Frontline staff interact with customers every day. They can answer key questions, like . . .

**What do our customers love about us? What frustrates them? What do they want us to fix?**

So often, companies don't give employees a voice to tap into that wealth of knowledge.

So your next questions may be: what is an employee engagement app, and what are the best practices to engage employees?

## What is an employee engagement app?

An employee engagement app is designed to create connected digital communities aligned around the company's mission, vision and values. It supports communication, collaboration and innovation by giving employees a voice. It encourages the exchange of ideas on improving both the customer and workplace experience. The result of effective employee engagement is higher customer and employee satisfaction, lower staff turnover, more efficient operations, and higher team productivity.

## 7 best practices to get the best from your employees

How do you get the best from your employees? Knowing how to retain, engage and motivate them starts with more communication and empathy. If you understand and act on what matters most to your staff, you'll see increases in customer satisfaction, employee satisfaction, team productivity and staff retention.

Getting the best from your employees requires a shift from a top-down "head office knows best" mindset to a bottom-up "we're here to support your success" mindset. Remember that culture change takes time. It won't happen overnight, but it will happen if you lay the groundwork and stay committed.

So how do you build a customer- and team-centric culture focused on amazing customer and workplace experiences? Follow these 7 steps.

### 1. Give employees a voice

Find out what's important to your employees. Measure your eNPS to find out how likely your staff are to recommend your company as a great place to work to their friends and colleagues. Keep making changes that give employees more of what they value.

### 2. Take a weekly team pulse check

Create a short 4-question team pulse survey. Ask operations-based questions like: Any customer requests or complaints this week? Anything you found frustrating? Tell us something that made you proud. Any suggestions for us? Improve employee satisfaction, engagement and retention by acting on the feedback.

### 3. Start a team feed to share ideas

Share customer feedback with frontline teams and ask them how they think the problem can be solved. Ask what barriers they see to providing a great customer experience. If they were making the decisions, what changes would they make? See what interesting ideas emerge.

### 4. Build connected digital communities

Create community profiles where employees share a bit about themselves with the team. Introduce new employees to their team members in the team feed. Share your company vision, values and goals as part of onboarding. Create a welcome video to strengthen the brand culture. Make it easy to message team members and managers.

### 5. Ask employees how they would improve the workplace

Acknowledge employees who are brave enough to give you input, whether or not you agree. Creating a culture where it's safe to share ideas takes time. It requires a period of building trust. Show teams there is no judgment and it's safe to put ideas forward.

### 6. Show employees they can make an impact

Find one suggestion a month you can implement and announce it to the team. Let them know they can make a difference by sharing their feedback. Thank the person whose idea was chosen, and acknowledge everyone for sharing their ideas.

### 7. Give kudos to scale desirable behaviours

Reward excellence. Give shout-outs to team members with outstanding performance. Acknowledge exemplary behaviours that reflect the company's core values. It may be an innovative idea, or staying late to help a customer or team member. Perhaps it's a community initiative or a charitable contribution.
