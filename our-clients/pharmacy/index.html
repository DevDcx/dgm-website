Case study · Pharmacy

Murphy's Pharmacies: fix what costs you customers before you step on the marketing gas

The challenge

Murphy's Pharmacies was losing customers and wanted to focus on personalized marketing. We suggested a different first step: look for the operational glitches that could undermine that marketing.

The team was open to it, but doubted customers would want to give feedback. So we ran a pilot across 7 stores to find out.

"Putting the gas on marketing before you fix the problems just means you lose more customers faster."
Dale Dubberley

How we got a 25% response rate

  1. 1Sent automatically from the POSA POS integration sent each comment card. The operations manager chose the frequency: one card per customer per month.
  2. 2Kept it to 1 or 2 questionsUnder 2 minutes to answer. We respected customers’ time instead of handing them a long survey.
  3. 3Rotated the questionsRolling questions across a high volume of cards gave the breadth of a full survey without alienating anyone.
  4. 4Said thank youEach response earned an entry to win a $50 pharmacy gift card.

What emerged: frontline intelligence they never had

Customers had been waiting to be asked. Every comment card brought problems to light that head office had no visibility into. It felt like whack-a-mole: find one, fix it, find the next, fix that.

The operations manager wondered if all these problems would cost him his job. But the problems were already there. They just weren't being seen. Now the team knew exactly what was costing them customers and sales, and could turn it around.
