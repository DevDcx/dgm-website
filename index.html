1. Connect with Customers

Customer Experience · Customer Engagement

Get customer feedback in real time. Send “come back” offers to increase return visits, promote good reviews and build loyalty engagement.

Increasing customer retention by 5% can raise profits by 25% to 95%. (Bain & Company, via Harvard Business Review)

2. Engage Employees

Employee Experience · Employee Accountability

Know how to attract and keep great staff. Get feedback from teams, resolve problems in real time, acknowledge success and use AI to spot training gaps.

Replacing a frontline employee costs about 16% of their annual salary. (Center for American Progress)

3. Personalize Marketing

Personalized Marketing · Marketing ROI

Send personalized offers that increase visits, spend and brand loyalty. Track the return on every promotion and boost sales with less discounting.

Personalization typically lifts revenue by 10% to 15%. (McKinsey & Company)

4. Optimize Operations

Team Productivity · Performance Intelligence

AI-driven performance management for every store. KPI alerts with prioritized actions, performance feedback and digital coaching, like a productivity coach for each manager.

Companies that make decisions from data are 5% to 6% more productive. (MIT Sloan research)
