What the AI actually does

AI works like a management coach and an extension of your team.

Customer Service Agent

Empowers your customers to find what they need fast and quickly connect with associates for more help. It handles low-skill tasks, so your team can spend more time with customers, and it collects valuable customer feedback.

HR Agent

Gives every new hire a positive onboarding experience with a personal learning plan. AI training avatars use role play to turn knowledge into skills. It checks in on team morale, so people are less likely to quit in the first 60 days.

Marketing Agent

Segments customers by value, sends personalized “come back” offers, and tracks the sales lift and return on every promotion, so you discount less and get more sales for every marketing dollar you spend.

Jane, Growth Coach

AI replaces dashboards with real-time operations intelligence. It alerts you when performance drops and identifies the likely causes when average basket size or repeat visits are falling. It recommends actions to increase sales and profits in every store, every week.
