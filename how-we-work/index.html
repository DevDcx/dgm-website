How we work

From your free Profit Growth Score to More Efficient Operations in 90 days

No one can honestly promise you more profit in 90 days. We can't control the economy, supply chains or tariffs. What we can do is help you improve the leading indicators of profit: the 8 Profit Drivers. We work alongside your team to agree on realistic targets, and strengthen the drivers that leave you most exposed to profit leaks first.

We don't want you to take our word for it. Each step delivers value that stands on its own, so you can decide to take the next one with confidence.

How we build your 90-day Profit Growth Plan

Your sales and margins are lagging indicators. The 8 Profit Drivers are the leading ones, so that's where we measure progress every week.

We use a simple decision matrix. Low-cost, high-impact actions come first. Higher-cost, high-impact actions come next, and these usually tie closely to the Brand Growth Strategy that gives you a lasting competitive edge.

At the end of 90 days, we repeat the process to find your next best opportunity, based on your new Profit Growth Score and what your customers and team are telling you.

Along the way, we usually find software you're paying for twice. Cutting those redundancies frees up your technology budget for what matters most: a better customer experience and more efficient operations.

Here's where the line sits. Sales and margins depend on things outside anyone's control. What we can do is strengthen the 8 Profit Drivers, the leading indicators that put your stores in the best position to grow sales and margins, whatever the market does. That's what we measure, and that's what we guarantee.
