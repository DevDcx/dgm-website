Our approach

How we build your 90-day plan

Sales, customer visits, average basket and customer value are lagging indicators. Review ratings, NPS, team pulse scores and offer engagement are leading ones. They show how well each Profit Driver is being managed, so that's where we measure progress every week.

We use a simple decision matrix. Low-cost, high-impact actions come first. Higher-cost, high-impact actions come next, and these usually tie closely to the Brand Growth Strategy that gives you a lasting competitive edge.

At the end of 90 days, we repeat the process to find your next best opportunity, based on your new Profit Growth Score and what your customers and team are telling you.

Along the way, we usually find software you're paying for twice. Cutting those redundancies frees up your technology budget for what matters most: a better customer experience and more efficient operations.

  • ✓More customers sharing feedback, while the experience is still fresh
  • ✓Customers more engaged with your brand and your offers
  • ✓More customers served at a lower cost, without sacrificing service

  • ✓Skills gaps in your frontline team, found and filled
  • ✓Individual learning plans so every team member can learn and grow at their own pace
  • ✓A healthier team culture, with lower turnover you can measure
  • ✓A higher return on every marketing dollar you spend
  • ✓One system that turns your data into intelligence for driving sales and profit
