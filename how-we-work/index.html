Questions about working with us

How fast will we see results?

Leading indicators like feedback, reviews and loyalty enrolment usually move within the first 90 days. Sales and margin follow, and our clients have seen the biggest financial gains over the first year.

Do we have to replace our systems?

No. Our platform connects to your existing POS and store systems, so your data comes together in one place.

What does the guarantee cover?

We agree on targets for the leading indicators of sales and profit before we start. If we miss them in the first 90 days, we work with you for another 90 days at no further cost.

Why start with 3 pilot stores?

It proves the result quickly, with less risk and less disruption. Once it works, we roll it out across the chain in waves of up to 3 stores a month.

Can we start without a big commitment?

Yes. The Profit Growth Score and the Profit Opportunity Review are free, and the Profit Growth Blueprint is $97. Every paid step is credited toward the next one.
