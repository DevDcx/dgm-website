Romers' Burger Bar: knowing what customers want before investing in growth

About Romers

When we met Kelly Gordon, Romers' Burger Bar had 4 locations, a 20% growth rate and aggressive plans for expansion. Their brand identity was "keeping it in the neighborhood". They sourced many ingredients locally, including 100% certified organic beef from local farmers, and had a great selection of local microbreweries on tap.

Kelly wanted to know what about the brand was most important to his customers before investing in growth. Customer insight had come from expensive market research firms, in monthly cycles and at an aggregated level, so managers were slow to respond to individual customers and loyal fans.
